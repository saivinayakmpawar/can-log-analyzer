Rules file format


    
Unknown keys are rejected. Counters and checksums are only checked when declared here; nothing is guessed.

  

  

    
What it checks

    

      
Signal limits
DBC minimum/maximum and your own thresholds. Merged into one event per excursion.

      
Message timing
Gaps, stopped and missing messages, frequency drift, using the DBC cycle time (or the log median, marked as an estimate).

      
Identifiers
11-bit and 29-bit IDs are kept apart. Unknown IDs and decode errors are listed.

      
Integrity
Rolling counters and checksums, for the signals and algorithms you declare.

      
Log quality
Backwards timestamps, lines the parser skipped, bus-load estimate with its assumptions.
